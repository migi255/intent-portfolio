# Portfolio content

- Consult the available master resume and evidence ledger. Use tailored applications to identify relevant angles, then trace factual claims back to their evidence.
- Record the contribution, outcome, and evidence limits. Preserve qualifiers such as approximate values, tested workloads, or evaluation size. Distinguish group outcomes from the user's own work.
- Draft unapproved details outside tracked files and site assets. Review the exact wording and obtain approval before publishing personal or confidential information.
- Put approved content in the shared content module. Inspect the rendered site and build output for accidental disclosure, including asset copies and metadata.
